Photography

Editorial and marketing photography takes .duotone — navy in the shadows, the orange spot warming the highlights, one soft wash instead of a literal press effect. Interface screenshots and product thumbnails stay untreated, just rounded to --radius-lg.

Usage

Use .duotone on hero and section imagery to keep the studio's real photography feeling like one brand, not a stock grab-bag. Never stack it on icons, diagrams or screenshots.
