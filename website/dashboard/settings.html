Idle-screen PIN

This is a privacy screen, not a login — your Cognito sign-in is still the real security boundary. Forgot your PIN? Use "Log out instead" on the locked screen, log back in, and save a new one — the old PIN isn't needed to replace it.

Stations

Stations are the production points a job is scheduled to. The code can't be changed once a station exists, because finished jobs and the audit trail already point at it — rename the display name instead. To take a station out of service, retire it: it disappears from the scheduling picker but jobs already on it keep running.
