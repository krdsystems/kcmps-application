Idle-screen PIN

A privacy deterrent, not a login — read the note below

When this dashboard auto-hides itself after sitting idle, it normally reopens with a plain "Resume" button. Setting a PIN here means your PIN has to be typed to reopen it instead — so a colleague walking past an unattended, still-logged-in screen can't casually see customer names, payment screenshots, or GCash references. This is not a real login — it's stored only in this browser and can't stop someone with technical access to this computer. The actual security boundary is your Cognito sign-in, which every action on this dashboard re-checks regardless of this PIN. If your session has genuinely expired, entering the right PIN will not "carry on" for you — you'll still be asked to refresh or log back in. Forgot your PIN? Log out and back in from the top bar — that always works and clears the old PIN.
