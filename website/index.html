Questions

Everything you might be wondering

How does a custom design request work?

Add “Custom design request” to your cart and check out — it's ₱0 up front. We review your idea, send you a quote, and only once you approve do you pay. No commitment, no surprise charges.

What files do you accept for my design?

PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG or AI — the higher resolution the better. Not sure if your file is print-ready? Send it anyway. We check and prep every file for free before we print.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum on ready-made shirts and prints — order a single piece if that's all you need. For bulk runs, use the estimator below for a directional quote.

How fast will I get it?

Ready-made designs are typically produced within the same or next business day. Custom work depends on the design — we'll tell you the turnaround when we send your quote.

How and when do I pay?

Ready-made priced items are settled when you place the order. Custom items are billed only after you approve the quote — we coordinate payment with you right after checkout.

Pickup or delivery?

Both. Pick up at our Manila studio, or ask for delivery at checkout and we'll arrange it.
